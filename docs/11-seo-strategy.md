# 11 — SEO Strategy

## Goal

Rank for buyers searching for Zoho consulting help — especially CEOs / Ops Heads in India and GCC evaluating CRM + operations partners.

SEO supports inbound P0/P1 leads, not traffic vanity.

## Keyword Pillars

### 1. Zoho consulting (core)

| Intent | Example queries |
| --- | --- |
| Partner search | Zoho consulting company, Zoho implementation partner India, Zoho partner GCC |
| Problem search | Zoho CRM not working, Zoho implementation failed, Zoho CRM low adoption |
| Service search | Zoho CRM consulting, Zoho One implementation, Zoho operations setup |

Primary money terms live on homepage, Capabilities, Approach, and Contact/Assessment paths.

### 2. Industry pages (priority growth surface)

One indexable page per ICP industry. Rank for industry + Zoho / CRM ops intent.

| Page | Target angles |
| --- | --- |
| `/industries/real-estate` | Zoho CRM for real estate, real estate lead management Zoho, channel partner follow-up CRM |
| `/industries/healthcare` | Zoho CRM for clinics/hospitals, patient enquiry management, appointment follow-up CRM |
| `/industries/education` | Zoho CRM for admissions, education enquiry management, counsellor tracking CRM |
| `/industries/service` | Zoho for service businesses, Zoho Desk + CRM handoff, SLA and delivery visibility |

Industry pages must describe operating problems, not list Zoho products.

### 3. Authority content (Insights)

Publish for problem + decision queries that precede partner selection.

Content types that fit AiDamsole:

- Why Zoho implementations fail after go-live
- CRM vs operations: where handoffs break
- How Ops Heads should evaluate a Zoho partner
- Industry playbooks (admissions funnel, site-visit tracking, ticket-to-account health)

Each article ends with a path to Talk to Expert / Assessment.

## Site Architecture for SEO

```
/                         → brand + Zoho consulting head terms
/industries/[slug]        → industry + Zoho intent
/insights/[slug]          → authority / problem intent
/approach                 → method / trust queries (if separated)
/contact or /assessment   → conversion (index selectively; no thin doorway pages)
```

Internal links:

- Homepage → all industry pages
- Insights → relevant industry + Capabilities/Approach
- Industry pages → Assessment / Talk to Expert

## Page Requirements

### Industry page

Must include:

1. Who it is for (company type + role)
2. Specific operating failures in that industry
3. What AiDamsole redesigns (CRM + ops)
4. Engagement expectation
5. Clear CTA

Reject: generic “We offer Zoho CRM, Books, Desk…” blocks.

### Authority article

Must include:

1. A real operating problem in the title
2. Practical judgment (what good looks like)
3. When to bring a consulting partner
4. CTA without soft-badge clutter

## Geographic Focus

| Region | Approach |
| --- | --- |
| India | Explicit in titles/copy where natural (“India”, city-agnostic first) |
| GCC | Separate mentions on industry/consulting pages; avoid fake city spam pages |

No mass location doorway pages (`/zoho-partner-dubai-1`, etc.).

## Measurement

| Signal | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Industry page → Talk to Expert rate | SEO quality for ICP |
| Insights → Assessment starts | Authority → intent |
| Query themes in Search Console | Double down on problem/partner terms |
| Qualified leads from organic (P0/P1) | True SEO success |

## Do Not Do

- Buy generic “Zoho partner” link packages
- Publish thin module synonym pages
- Chase global SaaS keywords unrelated to consulting acquisition
- Use blog volume targets without industry or failure-mode relevance
