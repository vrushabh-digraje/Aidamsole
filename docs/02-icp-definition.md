# 02 — Ideal Customer Profile

## Firmographic Fit

| Attribute | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Company size | 50–500 employees |
| Regions | India, GCC |
| Industries | Real Estate, Healthcare, Education, Service |
| Operating model | Multi-team operations with CRM, sales, service, or delivery workflows on (or moving to) Zoho |
| Maturity | Past spreadsheet / WhatsApp chaos; needs controlled systems, not another tool login |

## Who Buys

| Role | Why they care |
| --- | --- |
| CEO / Managing Director | Wants visibility into pipeline, delivery, and cash-linked operations without chasing reports |
| Head of Operations | Owns process discipline; loses time fixing broken handoffs between teams |
| Business / Practice Head | Accountable for revenue units that leak leads, SLAs, or follow-ups |
| IT / Systems Owner (influencer) | Evaluates Zoho fit, integrations, admin load, and vendor reliability |

Primary buyer: CEO or Ops Head.  
Secondary: Business Head. IT influences, rarely originates the engagement.

## Pain Points

### Cross-industry

- Zoho is licensed, but processes still live in Excel, chats, and tribal knowledge
- Modules were configured once, then abandoned — low adoption, no ownership
- Leadership cannot trust dashboards; numbers disagree across CRM, books, and ops
- Handoffs break between sales → delivery → support / accounts
- Previous vendor “implemented Zoho” without mapping how the business actually runs

### By industry

| Industry | Specific friction |
| --- | --- |
| Real Estate | Lead duplication across portals; site visits and broker follow-ups untracked; inventory / unit status disconnected from CRM |
| Healthcare | Patient / enquiry routing slow; appointment and follow-up leakage; compliance-sensitive data handled inconsistently |
| Education | Admission funnel poorly staged; counsellor accountability weak; fee / batch / enquiry data fragmented |
| Service | Ticket and project delivery not tied to account health; SLA breaches invisible until client escalates |

## Buying Triggers

- Scaling past founder-led follow-ups (50+ people, multiple locations or business units)
- New Zoho One / CRM / Desk purchase with no internal owner for process design
- Failed or stalled Zoho implementation by a prior partner
- Leadership demanding weekly pipeline / ops reviews and finding the data unusable
- Expansion into GCC or multi-city India operations exposing process gaps
- Audit, investor, or board pressure for controllable reporting
- Revenue leakage: missed leads, slow response, or unbilled delivery work

## Fit Signals

**Strong fit**

- Already on Zoho, or decided on Zoho and stuck at rollout
- Ops complexity across sales, service, and delivery
- Leadership willing to change process, not only install software
- Budget for consulting partnership, not one-week setup

**Poor fit**

- Under 50 people with no process owner
- Seeking cheapest Zoho “setup” or logo customization only
- Unwilling to define ownership, stages, and SOPs
- Pure product / SaaS build request outside Zoho consulting scope

## Engagement Expectation

ICP buyers expect a partner who:

1. Diagnoses how work actually moves across teams
2. Designs Zoho around operating reality, not default module layouts
3. Stays accountable after go-live — adoption, reporting, iteration
4. Speaks in business outcomes: control, speed, visibility, reduced leakage
